Type Splash is a bright 25-meter freestyle for kids learning home-row typing. One big letter at a time. No next-letter peeking. Correct keys swim him forward. Misses just make a bubbly bonk.

Laptop or desktop with a real keyboard. No phones, no touch.

How the race works

  • On your marks… get set… BEEP! Xavier dives in. The clock starts at the beep and stops on the 25th correct letter.
  • One letter, no peeking. A random home-row key — a s d f j k l ; — fills the pool. There is no next-letter preview.
  • Swim or bonk. The right key moves him one meter. A wrong key pops a gentle bubble. No extra penalties. The timer keeps running.
  • Hands and fingers. The on-screen keyboard lights the target key and names the hand and finger. F and J show the home-row bumps.
  • Ghost lanes. First race is a solo time trial. After that, up to three of his fastest swims replay beside him from the timestamp of each correct key.
  • Medals and PBs. Beat a ghost and earn a medal. Beat himself and the pool throws confetti for a new personal best.

Parents sign in. Kids just swim.

Focus signs in with Auth0. Xavier plays on that account — no extra kid login, no scores shared with anyone else. Each run is saved so tomorrow's ghosts are today's best times.
